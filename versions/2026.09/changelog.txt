# Meridian 2026.09

First release of Meridian (Survival 2027): the September 2026 pack.

## Downloads

- **Meridian-2026.09-powerful.mrpack**: the full pack, including the shader and resource packs hosted on Modrinth.
- **Meridian-2026.09-slowpc.mrpack**: the same mods without any shader or resource packs, for lower-end PCs.
- **Meridian-2026.09.mrpack**: no shader or resource packs (same as the slowpc build for now); the build uploaded to Modrinth.

## Notes

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.248. 8 GB or more RAM recommended.
- The first launch is slower while Sinytra Connector prepares Fabric mods.
- Shader packs and resource packs are not bundled in the files; powerful downloads the Modrinth-hosted ones at install.
- Back up your world before updating.
