# Meridian 2026.10

The October 2026 update: NeoForge 21.1.256, around 100 mod updates, a custom main menu and a few additions.

## Added

- FancyMenu with Drippy Loading Screen and Melody: custom main menu and loading screen.
- Analog Audio.
- Packet Fixer.

## Removed

- Better Advancements, Enhanced Boss Bars, PlayerMusic, ToolTipFix, Doors Achievement Sound and Translations for Sodium.
- All bundled shader packs and resource packs. Iris and Colorwheel stay, so you can add your own under Options > Video Settings > Shader Packs.

## Updated

- NeoForge 21.1.248 -> 21.1.256.
- Create Deep Seas 3.3.0, Mobile Networks 4.0.0, Furniture with Create and Sable compat 5.0, Sound Physics: Aeronautics 2.0.1.
- Distant Horizons 3.3.3, Chunksmith 4.4.0, WATERMeDIA 3.0.
- Sable 2.0.6, Create: LazyTick 2.7.30, Create: Dragons Plus 1.11.9, Exposure 1.9.19 and Exposure: Space 1.3.0.
- Most of the Let's Do mods, Supplementaries 3.9.9, Quark, Waystones, Simple Voice Chat 2.6.24 and many libraries.

## Downloads

- **Meridian-2026.10-powerful.mrpack**: the full pack.
- **Meridian-2026.10-slowpc.mrpack**: fewer mods for lower-end PCs. Left out: Antique Atlas and Antique Transport, Sound Physics (both), Entity Model Features, Entity Texture Features, Continuity, Polytone, FPS Reducer, LambDynamicLights, Create Dyn Light, Blur+, Inventory Blur, EnhancedVisuals and ImmersiveThunder.
- **Meridian-2026.10.mrpack**: the same mods as powerful; this is the build uploaded to Modrinth.

## Notes

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.256. 8 GB or more RAM recommended (6 GB is enough for slowpc).
- Back up your world before updating.
