# Meridian 2026.10

First public release of Meridian (Survival 2027). Updates ship monthly.

## Highlights

- Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge 21.1.248.
- Create with Steam 'n' Rails, Aeronautics, Sable physics, Big Cannons, Radar and Submarine.
- Let's Do food mods, Farmer's Delight and addons, Exposure photography, Simple Voice Chat.
- Sodium, Lithium, Distant Horizons and Iris with bundled shader packs.
- Modpack Update Checker: future updates show their changelog in game.

## Notes

- Recommended: 8 GB or more RAM allocated.
- The first launch is slower while Sinytra Connector prepares Fabric mods.
- Back up your world before updating to any later version.
