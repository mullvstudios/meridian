# Meridian 2026.11

NeoForge 21.1.265, about 65 mod updates, TFMG back in the pack and a cleanup of the performance-heavy extras.

## Added

- Create: TFMG Community Edition 1.3.2a.
- Fancy Entity Renderer.

## Removed

- Reliable EMI and TooManyRecipeViewers (EMI stays), Eating Animations, GroovyModLoader (GML) and Create Sable Dynamic Lights.

## Updated

- NeoForge 21.1.256 -> 21.1.265.
- Big version jumps: Supplementaries 4.0.2 (with Amendments 2.2.3 and Moonlight Lib 3.7.2), Friends & Foes 5.0.4, Create: Radars 5.0.2, Create Deep Seas 3.4.5, Create: Sound of Steam 0.9.2.1, Polytone 5.3.2, Vanilla Backport 1.2.2, Cubes Without Borders 5.0.0, Platform 1.4.2, Immersive Paintings 0.8.0, Almanac 1.26.10.3.
- Chunksmith 4.8.0, Simple Voice Chat 2.6.27, Forgified Fabric API 2.3.6, Mobile Networks 4.1.0, ModernFix 5.27.27, C2ME alpha 124, LightSpeedRe 2.0.5, Sodium Extra 0.9.5, Sound Physics Remastered 1.5.2, Exposure: Space 1.4.0 and Quark 4.1-488.
- Let's Do: Vinery 1.5.6, Furniture 1.1.10, Farm & Charm 1.1.29, Meadow 1.4.10, Hearth & Timber 1.0.5, WilderNature 1.1.8, Camping 2.1.6 and their addons (Bakery, Brewery, Candlelight, Corn Expansion, Apple Wood Rebarked).
- Many smaller library and fix updates.

## Download

- **Meridian-2026.11.mrpack**: the full pack, also the build uploaded to Modrinth.

## Notes

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265. 8 GB or more RAM recommended.
- Servers must update to the same mod versions and NeoForge 21.1.265.
- Back up your world before updating.
